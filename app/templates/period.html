💡 Period Care Tips

  • 🥤 Stay hydrated - drink plenty of water to reduce bloating
  • 🏃 Light exercise can help reduce cramps and improve mood
  • 🌡️ Use a heating pad for cramp relief
  • 😴 Get adequate rest - your body needs extra sleep during your period
  • 🥗 Eat iron-rich foods to replenish nutrients
  • 🧘 Practice relaxation techniques to manage stress and pain
  • 📝 Track your symptoms to identify patterns and discuss with your doctor
